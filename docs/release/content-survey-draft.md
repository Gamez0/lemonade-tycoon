# Content Survey preparation

Prepared2026-10-13 from the maintained reboot source, development history and
[Valve's Content Survey guide](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey).
These are draft answers for later owner review, not submitted ratings or approvals.

## Content inventory

The current single-player game manages fictional lemonade stock, recipes, prices,
customers and upgrades. Source inspection found no sexual content, graphic violence,
real-money transactions, loot boxes, chat, user uploads or multiplayer. General and
mature-content answers should be checked against the final package and all bundled
content when completing Steam's actual questionnaire; no regional age rating is
claimed here.

## AI-assisted content draft

Development used an AI coding assistant, including player-facing procedural art,
icons and score/synthesis sources. Code-generated visuals and sounds should be
included in the authoring inventory, rather than assumed exempt because their
maintained assets are code. Candidate disclosure:

> AI-assisted development was used to author procedural artwork and audio
> score/synthesis sources. Those visuals and sounds are included in the game.
> The shipped game does not generate content using AI models or connect to AI
> services during play.

This classification follows Valve's distinction between pre-generated content
created during development and live model generation during play. The maintained
runtime has no model client/service integration; procedural NPC rules, drawing and
audio synthesis are ordinary game code. Final questionnaire wording and contributor
authoring history require owner review at submission. Distribution-rights evidence
remains in [the register](rights-register.md).
