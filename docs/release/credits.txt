Willow Lane Lemonade - development candidate

Game rules, generated street/character art, UI icons, capsule composition,
pentatonic music miniatures and synthesized effects were written for this project.
Music source: src/game/presentation/audio.ts. No original-game recording, image
or third-party musical composition is included in the reboot PC distribution.
Human listening acceptance and contributor distribution-rights confirmation
remain release checks. The separate legacy preview is excluded from this package.

Neighborhood houses, tree and hedge: generated with OpenAI image generation from
project-owned structural studies, then reviewed at the game's logical resolution.
Their prompts and provenance are in art/studies/neighborhood. No original-game
pixels were used as generation inputs or copied into these raster assets.

Basic, Classic, Lemon and Castle stand sprites: independently generated with
OpenAI image generation from project-owned references. Original alpha files,
exact prompts and source bounds are retained in art/studies/stand-upgrades.
These sprites are separate from every map background and follow saved ownership.

Ten location backgrounds: independently generated with OpenAI image generation
from project-owned studies and written art direction. Prompts and selections:
art/studies/neighborhood-cohesion and art/studies/all-map-style. Original-game
reference images were reviewed privately, never supplied as generation inputs
or copied into these assets. Pedestrian routes and stand anchors are authored
separately in src/game/content/map-layouts.ts.
