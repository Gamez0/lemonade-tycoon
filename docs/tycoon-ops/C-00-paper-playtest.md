# C-00 paper playtest kit — unapproved hypotheses
Three candidates from L-006; numbers are invented test fixtures, not measured demand. Use 3–5 minutes per candidate, rotate order across testers, no ads or real-time waiting. Record actual choices, explanation, elapsed time, errors and desire to replay; do not fill results before testing.

## A — neighborhood cookie workshop
Start cash 20, two oven slots, three rounds. Each slot produces 4 cookies, costs 4; sold cookies earn 3, leftovers expire after one additional round. Demand (chocolate/butter) round 1: 5/2; round 2: 2/5; round 3: 4/4. Reveal next round only after current choices. Choose slot allocation each round, whether to retain leftovers, and whether to spend 8 on a third slot after round 1. Example round 1: 4/4 production sells 4/2, revenue 18, cash 30, two butter left; 8/0 sells 5, revenue 15, cash 27, three chocolate left. Test whether inventory forecast and upgrade timing matter without waiting.

## B — custom order bakery
Start cash 20, oven capacity 2 batches per round. Each batch costs 4. Orders: round 1 express chocolate (1 batch, pays 9, due now) and party assortment (2 batches, pays 22, due round 2); round 2 butter rush (1 batch, pays 10, due now); round 3 premium assortment (2 batches, pays 24, due now). Accept or decline each order, allocate slots, decide whether to spend 6 for one extra slot in one round. Late orders pay zero, no negative cash allowed. Example: express + one party batch in round 1 leaves cash 21 and one party batch outstanding; round 2 complete party and rush for cash 45. Alternative prioritize party early, lose express opportunity but free round 2 capacity. Test whether scheduling is understandable without dense controls.

## C — cookie brand management
Start cash 20, production capacity 8 units per round, unit cost 1. Two products: classic and premium. Local channel pays 2/unit, online pays 3/unit but costs 3 per round used. Demand caps by channel (classic/premium): round 1 local 5/2, online 2/4; round 2 local 2/5, online 4/2; round 3 local 4/4, online 3/3. Choose product mix, channel allocation, whether to spend 8 on +4 capacity after round 1. Unsold units expire. Example round 1 produce 5 classic/3 premium, local sells 5 classic, online sells 3 premium: revenue 19 less production 8 and online fee 3, cash 28. Local-only 5 classic/2 premium yields cash 27 if producing seven. Test whether channel tradeoff is legible rather than spreadsheet work.

## Decision gate
For each candidate record at least three consequential choices, whether alternatives yield distinct outcomes, whether recovery after a poor first round is possible, whether a player can explain the loop without help, and replay interest (1–5). Fail if choices are dominated, waiting is the only action, or a tester cannot complete within five minutes. Compare actual observations, not fixture scores. Independent reviewer should check arithmetic, dominance and mobile interaction burden before any user direction approval. No C-01 commitment.
