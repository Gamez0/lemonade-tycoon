# Ten playable locations

The seven additional themes join the rental catalogue, paid daily contracts,
saved local ratings, replay, results and world rendering. All ten backgrounds
have separate stand sprites and authored routes. The rental strip scrolls
horizontally with three cards visible, preserving the details and action area.

New locations share Downtown's existing seven-day / $120 lifetime sales / 65%
satisfaction milestone. Historical saves retain their earned unlock list;
subsequent completed business can earn the new venues through the normal rules.
Save version9 fills neutral ratings for the seven venues without changing old
cash, contracts, equipment or replay. Version0-8 imports remain supported.

These are remake tuning values, not recovered original economic formulas.
The original audit (docs/research/original-116-live-audit.md) records relative
rents and qualitative audiences. New rents use one fifth of those original
amounts, fitting the existing reboot's lower prices: Station $6, Beach $8,
Mall/Marina $10, Old Square/Magic Gardens $20, Stadium $30. Moving costs are half
the new rent. Traffic, budgets, three-profile mixtures, patience and arrival
intervals are explicit new balancing choices. Existing three-location economics
are unchanged. Eleven original customer identities and Mall's original indoor
temperature/weather distinction are still separate fidelity work; no exact
original simulation claim is made here.

Validation must include old-save migration, every venue's paid replay and local
ratings, all forty stand/map combinations, narrow-window rental navigation and
moving customer captures. Human visual acceptance remains open in #97.
